# Evolution

- A game mode of LAN Play on any map (button **Evolution** in the menu **LAN Play** of the room, gadget `evo_game.lua` and game widget `evo_gift.lua` of the extra game "Level Editor Scenarios").
- Every team plays one unit that evolves through 10 stages by kills.
- **Files:** `evo_game.lua` is the synchronized part (about 2,100 lines); the unsynced part (score lines, panels of the abilities and the experience bar, Armageddon, the missile targets, the selection, the camera, the heal effect) is `evo_ui.lua` (`luaui/Include/evo_ui.lua`, in `$ScenarioGameFiles`): `evo_game.lua` calls it with `(gadget, { NAMES, LADDERS, ABILITIES, teamName })`, the lists and names that both parts use.
- User view: [Evolution manual](../LevelEditor/game-modes/evolution.md).

## 1. LAN Play room (`lan_lobby.lua`)

- Button **Evolution** in the menu of the button **LAN Play** (title row of the room, see [LAN Play](lan-play.md); caption always "Evolution", its class green when on, grey when off; it opens the submenu, see [LAN Play](lan-play.md)), not in a scenario room.
  - The host switches it (`setEvolution`): the game option `lanevolution=1` in `S.battle.modoptions` (sent to the room with `SETSCRIPTTAGS game/modoptions/lanevolution=1`, empty when off), and `syncPveGame()` because the mode needs the extra game (`needsExtraGame`).
  - A joiner sees the state from the game options of the room and cannot change it.
- Switching it on opens a window for the host (`bigPopup(EVO_INFO)`): the rules in short and the tip about the map size (size class M or smaller, up to 16 x 16; the size classes of the lobby: M is mostly 16 x 16).
- Choosing a scenario switches it off (a scenario has its own rules).
- Start script (`buildScript`): `lanevolution=1`, `deathmode=neverend` instead of the room's value (the commanders are replaced, the game must not end for that; the mode never ends), `experimentallegionfaction=1` and `experimentalextraunits=1` (Legion and the Extra Units Pack always on, whatever the room says; `EVO_FORCED`; LAN_VERSION 20) and `StartPosType=0` (the start positions of the map: choosing a position would only hold up the start).
- Test automation: `evolution=1` in `lanlobby_cfg.txt` (the host switches it on after 10 s); `botVersion=` for the computer of `bot=`.
- Changing these rules is a change of the LAN logic: `LAN_VERSION` 16.

## 2. Gadget `evo_game.lua`

- Loaded only with the game option `lanevolution=1` (otherwise the file returns false).
- **Players:** from frame 30 to 900, every 15 frames, every team (not Gaia, not dead) with a commander (`customParams.iscommander`) joins: its units are removed (no wreck, no explosion) and a stage 1 unit stands where the commander stood.
  - Teams without a commander (Scavengers, Raptors) stay as they are.
  - A team without an active human player (`Spring.GetPlayerList(team, true)`, not a spectator) is a computer team.
- **Long range (game option `lanevolong=1`):** `LONG` (level -> names: 3 armsnipe, legsrail; 4 cordeadeye; 5 armmanni, corsiegebreaker; 8 legerailtank; 9 corthermite, legpede; 10 legapollyon; the units with `Long range = yes` of the table) are added by `stageUnit` to the options of their level when `LONG_RANGE`; without the option the ladder only.
  - Level of a unit of the table: `OVERRIDE` in `evolution_units.py` (Telchine on 7).
- **Unit candidates:** `game-modes/evolution-units.md` (linked at the end of `game-modes/evolution.md`; the same as `game-modes/evolution_units.csv` for Excel) lists every mobile unit of Armada, Cortex and Legion (431; the Scavenger and Raptor copies are not listed; 77 are candidates, they come first by level (the column Included marks the 30 that are in the game now), the others have no level and the column Note says why they are left out) with type, tech level, DPS (default armor, without anti air, paralyzers, D-gun, stockpile weapons), health, metal, speed, range and a proposed level: the level whose `LADDERS` units have the closest DPS x health (logarithmic scale).
  - Reasons for leaving out (the column Note): rockets without homing (a main weapon of the type missile / starburst without `tracks`: for 1:1 fights only homing missiles are fine, like Karganeth and Torch; the units of the first version are kept), too slow (speed below 31), the dps of torpedoes is not counted (water only), constructors, aircraft, artillery and rocket launchers (the description says so, or the main weapon, not counting stockpiled starburst missiles, is a cannon or missile of 1000 Elmo or more), mine layers, water units, units without a ground weapon (radar, jammers, spies, anti air, crawling bombs), the Epic and Archaic variants, Tumbleweeds, commanders, drones;
    - the scouts (DPS x health below 10000: Tick, Rover, Rascal, Wheelie, Goblin) and the weak support vehicles (DPS below 40: Whistler, Lasher, Lance, Satyr, Belcher).
  - **The csv is the source** for the levels: change *Level* (a number = in the game), *Long range* and *Note* in `game-modes/evolution_units.csv` (Excel) and double-click `game-modes/update_evolution_units.bat` (`evolution_units.py update`): it writes the ranges of the levels and the table of all units into `evolution-units.md` (**only between the comment lines `BEGIN TABLE` and `END TABLE`**, in front of the heading "The ranges of the levels"; the text above stays) and the block between `-- BEGIN POOL` and `-- END POOL` of `evo_game.lua`.
  - Made new from the numbers of the game by `test-data\generators\evolution_units.py` from the log of `pve_test.ps1 -Evo -Widget zz_unitdump.lua` (the Level and Long range of units that are in the csv are kept) (one `[zzunit]` line per unit).
  - The ladders are to be drawn from this table.
- **Units:** `POOL` (between `-- BEGIN POOL` and `-- END POOL` in `evo_game.lua`, made by `test-data\generators\evolution_units.py` from the table of `game-modes/evolution-units.md`: change the table and run the script, not the block): per level the 77 candidates of Armada, Cortex and Legion (`POOL_LONG`: the 9 units with a long range, only with the game option `lanevolong`);
  - `stageUnit` takes a random faction of those that have a unit on the level (every faction the same chance), then a random unit of it that exists in `UnitDefNames`.
  - The three units per level of `LADDERS` (the first ten of each faction, chosen by DPS x health) are part of the pools and keep their level; `LADDERS` is used by the help window as the examples.
- **Points:** `need(s) = max(1, floor(3 * 1.6^(s - 1) + 0.5))` from stage s to s + 1.
  - A wild unit or tower of stage s gives `wildWorth(s) = max(1, floor(need(s) / 3 + 0.5))` (about three for the next stage);
    - every kill (wild or player) counts `1 + R.CATCH_UP x (highest stage of the players who are in - stage of the killer)` times, `R.CATCH_UP` = 0.5 (a player on stage 1 next to one on stage 5 gets three times the points), a player unit of stage v killed by a unit of stage k gives `need(v) * max(1, 1 + v - k)` (`worth`).
  - Points go to the team of the attacker (`UnitDestroyed` attackerTeam), never for an allied or the own unit.
  - Several stages at once are possible; at stage 10 the points stay 0.
- **Experience bar:** team rule `evoExp` (`p.exp`);
  - the own panel (`H.character`) has the points bar and under it a violet experience bar ("XP 0.3", fill `exp / (exp + 1)`);
  - the overview at the right has no bars: one line with the points now and for the next level ("4/12", MAX at the top) and "XP 0.3", kills above deaths in a column left of the abilities, the name shortened to its room (`gl.GetTextWidth`).
  - In the buttons of the abilities a name of two words stands in two lines (Radar / Pulse).
  - The text of Armageddon ("ARMAGEDDON IS HERE") and "Taking n/5" scale with `H.scale()` like everything else.
- **Window of the gifts** (`evo_gift.lua`): three buttons with their own colors and symbols drawn with plain shapes: Hell on Earth red with a flame, Heaven on Earth blue with wings and a halo, Gates of Hell dark yellow with the radiation symbol;
  - the name without number (the keys 1, 2, 3 still work);
  - the same colors in the help window (`H.help`);
  - the debug button Armageddon has the symbol (`H.atom`).
- **Experience:** a kill of a player unit gives the killer's unit +`EXP_STEP` = 0.1 unit experience (`Spring.SetUnitExperience`; the game's own bonus for health, damage and reload), a death costs 0.1 (not below 0); the value is kept in `p.exp` and given to every new unit (`spawn`), so it stays through level ups and respawns; wild units give none.
- **Evolving:** the new unit replaces the old one at its place (`evolve`: position, facing, health: the health fraction of the old unit plus 20 %, at most full; a human's unit keeps up to 16 orders, given again with shift).
- **Land:** at the start `buildLand` makes a grid of 128 x 128 Elmo cells where a heavy tank (Bull) can stand (`Spring.TestMoveOrder` without objects) and takes the connected piece (8 neighbours) with the most commanders (on a tie the bigger one; the list of its cells is sorted, so it is the same on every computer).
  - Every unit spawns on it (`randomLand`): nobody is cut off by water or cliffs.
  - A commander off that piece (another island) has its first unit put somewhere on it.
- **Death before Armageddon:** the stage goes down by one (at least 1), the points are lost, deaths + 1; after 5 s the unit comes back at `spawnPoint`: of 24 random places on the land where the unit can stand (also no unit or feature in the way) the one farthest from the units of the players.
- **Armageddon:** the first unit on stage 10 (`reachTop`): `Spring.SetGlobalLos(allyTeam, true)` for every ally team (no fog of war), the game rules `evoArmageddon` (frame) and `evoHero`, two chat lines; the unsynced part shows "ARMAGEDDON IS HERE" in big letters for 10 s and "ARMAGEDDON" in the title line.
  - From then on a destroyed player unit does not come back: the player is out (`evoOut`), the units of its gifts blow up.
  - **The alliance against level 10** (`setCoalition`, when a player reaches stage 10 and when one is out): as long as a player on stage 10 is in, every alliance of the start without such a player is allied with every other one of them (`Spring.SetAlly` both ways; chat line "Everybody else is allied now against ...");
    - when no player on stage 10 is left the alliances of the start come back ("... everybody fights on their own again").
  - Targets of the computer players and of the gifts and the points use `enemies(p, q)` (not the same alliance and not `AreTeamsAllied`).
  - The last alliance of the start with a player that is not out wins (`checkWin`: `evoState` = "won by ...", `Spring.GameOver`).
  - Others may still reach stage 10 later.
- **Gifts of stage 10:** every player who reaches stage 10 (`reachTop`, also later ones) chooses one (`evoGift` = 1 while it may): the game widget `evo_gift.lua` shows a window with three buttons (keys 1, 2, 3) and sends `evo|gift|<n>` (`RecvLuaMsg` checks the team of the player); a computer player chooses at once, a human gets a random one after 30 s.
  - 1 **Hell on Earth**: 8 tier 3 ground units (stage 8 to 10, random factions, `hellUnit`) at random places of the land at least 900 Elmo from the enemies, for the player, ;
    - a unit that is lost is replaced by a new one somewhere (`hellOf`, `pending`, counted in `UnitDestroyed`, spawned by `hellStep`), and one more comes every `HELL_GAP` = 30 s, until the game is won or the player is out (measured with `zz_hell.lua`: after destroying all of them with the cheat command the number came back at once, then one more per 900 frames).
  - 2 **Heaven on Earth**: 8 Dragons (`corcrw`, heavy gunships; without it in the game Hell on Earth) evenly spread on a hexagon (corners every 60 degrees, 320 Elmo from the unit) round the player's unit;
    - a lost one is replaced by a new one near the player's unit (`heavenOf`, `heavenStep`), and one more comes at a random place of the land every `HEAVEN_GAP` = 30 s (measured with `zz_hell.lua -Gift 2`).
  - 3 **Gates of Hell**: nuclear missiles (the weapon of armsilo / corsilo / legsilo): first a barrage at once (`giftGates`: `GATES_PER_PLAYER` = 10 per player, at least 20, all after the 5 s warning at random places of the land, not near the units of the one who chose it or his alliance, so the number depends on the players, not on the size of the map), then they never stop (`gates`, `gatesStep` every update until the game is won or the player is out): every human enemy has missiles of his own: a volley at him every 10 s at the start that becomes 3 s over four minutes (`10 - 7 t / 240`), of 1 + t / 120 s missiles (one at first, two after two minutes, then one more every two minutes), at first 2000 Elmo from his unit (+ up to 200), 12 Elmo less per second down to 120, so they come closer and closer (computer players are not followed, except with the test option `lanevotest`);
    - besides that one missile at a random place of the land at every volley rhythm of the gate (the map fills up);
    - never within `NUKE_SAFE` = 900 Elmo of a unit of the player who chose the gate or of his alliance (measured with `zz_gates.lua`: the enemy's unit was destroyed after 116 s, the player's unit was not hit);
    - each one is shown 5 s before by red rings (`evoNukes`, drawn by the unsynced part) and comes down with `Spring.SpawnProjectile` from 2500 Elmo above (`Spring.SpawnExplosion` when that fails).
  - The kills of the gifts count for the player.
  - Units of a gift of a computer team attack the nearest enemy player unit.
  - The gifts 1 and 2 (the units with heavy lasers) set `p.giftEnergy`: `giveEnergy` then adds `R.GIFT_ENERGY` = 1000 energy per second for good (the storage stays `R.STORE`).
  - `giveGift` writes "<name> chose <gift>!" and one of the three sayings of that gift (`GIFT_SAYINGS`, at random) into the chat.
- **Rules table `R`** (Lua 5.1 allows only so many locals): energy, wild units, towers, radars below.
- **Energy:** every second (`giveEnergy`) every player team that is not out gets `R.ENERGY_BASE` = 5 + `R.ENERGY` = 3 per stage (stage 1: 8, 8: 29; `R.STORE` = 1000, `R.STORE_BIG` = 2000 on stage 10 (a bonus) and with the game option Long range (`LONG_RANGE`; `giveEnergy` raises the storage): the beam weapons of that option cost up to 1500 per shot (Siegebreaker 1500, Starlight 1000, Deadeye 800; measured with `zz_unitdump.lua`); the beams of Titan (1300) and Juggernaut (1200) cost more than 1000, too, but those two are on the normal ladder: their beams can be fired on level 10, where the storage is 2000) (`AddTeamResource`; a Razorback of stage 8 needs 75 energy per shot, 750 per second when it fires without a pause: the rest has to come from the generators);
  - the rest comes from the solar collectors and wind turbines below and a storage of at least `R.STORE` = 1000 (`SetTeamResource "es"`; the commander and its storage are gone, laser weapons need energy).
- **Units that cloak by themselves and the generators:** a unit whose definition can cloak (Gremlin, Sharpshooter) is not allowed to: `gadget:UnitCreated` puts it in `cloakers`, `noCloak` (every second) sets `SetUnitCloak` false and the cloak order off unless the power-up Cloak works, and `AllowCommand` refuses the order to cloak (the order alone did not switch the state off in the test: `zz_nocloak.lua`).
  - `generatorsOn` (every second) turns the solar collectors and wind turbines of `radars` on again and `AllowCommand` refuses `CMD.ONOFF` 0 for them.
- **Radar from level 8:** `revealTop` (every second) sets for the unit of a player on stage `R.REVEAL_STAGE` = 8 to 9 (not in Armageddon, not while the power-up Cloak works) the radar and contact bits of the line of sight state for every other ally team (`SetUnitLosMask` / `SetUnitLosState` 10): the unit shows on the radar and the minimap of everybody;
  - the room says "... shows on the radar of everybody now: ally against it!" once (`p.onRadar`);
  - when the condition ends the mask is cleared.
  - Test: `pve_test.ps1 -Evo -EvoTest -Debug -Widget zz_reveal.lua` (lines `test: ... on the radar of ally team ...: true`).
- **All Be Gods (game option `lanevogods=1`, the room's button, default off):** `ALL_GODS`;
  - `reachTop`: the first player on stage 10 (`godPlayer`) brings Armageddon and may choose a gift;
  - another player who reaches stage 10 later is in the coalition against level 10 as usual, but without the option gets no `giftAt` and the chat says "... reached level 10 as well, but too late: the special power belongs to the first one.";
  - with the option every player on stage 10 chooses a gift (the former behavior).
  - Test: `pve_test.ps1 ... -Gods`.
- **Automatic reclaim (`autoReclaim`, every second):** the unit of a player reclaims the features (trees, wrecks; `FeatureDefs[..].reclaimable`) within `R.RECLAIM_RANGE` = 100 Elmo when no unit of an enemy player is within `R.RECLAIM_ENEMY` = 700, at most `R.RECLAIM_RATE` = 60 energy a second and, as health, 1 % of the maximum health (`R.RECLAIM_HEAL_PERCENT`, at least `R.RECLAIM_HEAL_MIN` = 1, at most `R.RECLAIM_HEAL_MAX` = 150); the work is done by `G.reclaimFeatures` of `led_gadgets.lua` (also used by Renegade): a resource without room is left out, so a unit at full health still takes the energy of a wreck; the energy of a feature goes into the team energy 1 to 1 (`AddTeamResource`) only while the storage has room;
  - the metal of a feature heals the unit 1 to 1 (`SetUnitHealth`) only while it is hurt (no metal goes into the metal storage);
  - `Spring.SetFeatureReclaim` lowers what is left of the feature, `DestroyFeature` when it is used up;
  - a full storage or full health means nothing is taken.
  - Test hook `evo|test|6` (hurts the unit to 30 % and puts a wreck `armpw_dead` next to it).
- **Radar sayings:** `RADAR_SAYINGS` (six lines, `%s` = the name of the player) at random when a player begins to show on the radar (`revealTop`); the power-ups Cloak and Jammer (`p.active`) switch the signal off while they work.
- **Quick start:** `LevelEditor\start_evolution.bat [map] [AI]` (default `Hooked 1.1` and `SimpleAI`) runs `ensure_installed.ps1` and then `scripts\start_evolution.ps1`, which writes `LevelEditor\evolution_script_installed.txt` (ignored by git) and starts the engine on it: the player in team 0, the computer player in team 1 (each in an ally team of its own, both Legion), the extra game of `data\games\LevelEditorScenarios.txt`, game options `lanevolution=1; lanevodebug=1; lanplay=1; deathmode=neverend; experimentallegionfaction=1; experimentalextraunits=1`.
- **Regeneration:** every `REGEN_STEP` = 30 frames the unit of a player that is in gets `REGEN` = 0.2 % of its health (1 % in 5 s), but only with `REGEN_ON` (game option `lanevoregen` not 0, the Evolution menu of the room) and not for `REGEN_WAIT` = 150 frames (5 s) after the last damage (`UnitPreDamaged` sets `p.hurtAt`); not for wild units.
- **Wild units:** `wildCount()` = one per 1024 x 1024 Elmo of the land, 12 to 100;
  - Gaia units at random places, health at the start by its level (`wildHp(stage)`: 10 % on level 1, 30 % on levels 2 to 4, 40 % on levels 5 to 7, 50 % from level 8; the game option `lanevohp` 10 to 100 of the Evolution menu sets one value for all levels) (`SetUnitHealth`: `SetUnitHealth` to half of the maximum, the maximum stays, so the health bar shows 50 %; measured with `zz_wildhp.lua`), fire state return fire, move state hold position.
  - `wild[id] = { stage, tower }`.
  - The stage (`wildStage`): the lowest stage of the players who are not out (the weakest player can always catch up on them).
  - A wild tower does `R.TOWER_DAMAGE` = 50 % of its damage (`UnitPreDamaged`).
  - `R.STILL` = 20 % of the wild units never walk (`wild[id].still`, the towers always).
  - Every `R.WANDER` = 5 s `wanderWild` sends each other idle wild unit with the chance `R.WANDER_SHARE` = 60 % to a random place of the land 210 to 700 Elmo away (`CMD.MOVE`; they keep holding their position there and only shoot back).
  - With the chance `R.TOWER` = 15 % a spawn is a tower (`stageTower`: `R.TOWERS`, three lists of 10 towers per faction, ground targets, ordered by cost; anti air, nuclear and anti nuclear buildings left out; `fits` uses `TestBuildOrder` for buildings).
  - A destroyed one comes back 10 s later somewhere else (a new stage); every `R.CHECK` = 10 s `topUpWild` fills up to `wildCount()` (living ones plus those due; ids that are gone without `UnitDestroyed` are dropped).
  - Every `R.UPGRADE` = 3 s `upgradeWild` replaces one wild unit (not one that a player sees (`IsUnitInLos` of the ally team of a player that is in) or that is within `R.SAFE` = 900 Elmo of a player's unit: `watched`; no change of level in the middle of a fight; the test option logs `replaced` / `kept` counts) above that stage at all (the weakest player must have a chance, also after a death) or two or more stages below it with one of that stage at its place (a tower with a tower).
- **Solar collectors and wind turbines:** at the start `placeGenerators`: `R.GENERATORS` of level 1 (armsolar, corsolar, legsolar, armwin, corwin, legwin that exist; a random one each) at random places of the land, at least 300 Elmo apart, one per `R.GEN_AREA` = 1536 x 1536 Elmo, `R.GEN_MIN` = 6 to `R.GEN_MAX` = 30;
  - neutral Gaia units in the table `radars` (created by `newRadar`), so they cannot be destroyed and are taken like the radars (chat "... took a solar collector / a wind turbine.");
  - they give their energy to the team that holds them.
  - They do not become T2.
- **Radars:** at the start `placeRadars`: neutral radars of Gaia (`R.RADAR1`, a random faction; `SetUnitNeutral`, nobody shoots at them on its own) until `R.COVER` = 95 % of the land cells are in their range (the radar's `radarDistance`, 2100);
  - each new one stands in a random cell no radar reaches yet, so they keep their distance and cover the land evenly (at most `R.MAX_RADARS` = 60).
  - They cannot be destroyed (`UnitPreDamaged` returns 0; `GG.Indestructible` of `unit_indestructible.lua` when loaded).
  - Every 15 frames `takeRadars`: units of exactly one alliance of players within `R.RANGE` = 200 Elmo and not the owner's: after `R.TAKE` = 5 s the radar goes to the team of the unit that began (`TransferUnit`, chat line "... took a radar."); another alliance there or nobody resets it.
  - The progress is the game rule `evoTaking` ("x,z,seconds,team" separated by /), drawn by the unsynced part as "Taking 3/5" in the taker's color above the radar.
  - A player who is out gives their radars back to Gaia (`freeRadars`).
  - When one player who is in is on stage `R.T2_STAGE` = 6 or higher, `upgradeRadars` replaces one building every 3 s with its T2 version (`R.RADAR2`: radar -> advanced radar (range 3500), solar collector -> advanced solar collector, wind turbine -> `*wint2`) for the same owner, at its place; when no player is on that stage any more the buildings go back to T1 (`R.BACK1`).
  - A building that a player sees or that is within `R.SAFE` = 900 Elmo of a player's unit (`watched`) is not changed.
  - The T2 buildings give more energy to the team that owns them.
  - Test: `pve_test.ps1 -Evo -EvoStage 6 -EvoTest -Debug -Widget zz_bld.lua` (lines `test: building armrad -> armarad`).
- **Abilities and beacons** (table `A`; `ABILITIES` at the top is shared with the unsynced part): five abilities in this order (key, name, color, energy cost, seconds it lasts, cooldown; the order is the column in the panel and the index of the button): Sprint (blue, 200, 8 s, 30 s: `MoveCtrl.SetGroundMoveTypeData` maxSpeed / maxWantedSpeed x 1.5), Repair (green, 500, at once, 60 s: +20 % health (`A.HEAL`); at full health `A.use` does nothing and spends no energy), Radar Pulse (orange, 250, 5 s, 45 s: los and airLos 2500), Cloak (yellow, 400, 15 s, 60 s: `SetUnitCloak` true with decloak distance 75 and script cloak 1, which the engine ignores for a unit whose definition has `canCloak = false` (measured: `cloaked false` while the ability is active); so `A.hide` also forces the line of sight state of the unit for every other ally team to nothing with `SetUnitLosMask(id, ally, 15)` and `SetUnitLosState(id, ally, 0)` (measured with an enemy unit 120 Elmo away: before `los true radar true`, during the ability nothing, for the whole 15 s); it ends with `A.hide(id, false)`; the tooltip of the button says so (`info` of the entry in `ABILITIES`, an extra line: "Hold Fire is set while you are cloaked, Fire at Will after it: flee from battle"); the unit of a human player is put on hold fire (`CMD_FIRE_STATE` 0, `p.holdFire`) and gets a stop order when the cloak starts (without the stop the weapons kept firing at the target they had: measured with `zz_cloak.lua`), and for the first 60 frames `act.grace` a shot only updates the baseline `act.base` of the reload frames (a shot just before the cloak does not end it) and back on fire at will (2) when it ends; a computer player keeps its fire state; firing ends it: every 15 frames `A.update` looks at the `reloadFrame` of the weapons of the unit and stops the cloak (chat line "... is seen") when one began to reload since the last look), Jammer (violet, 300, 20 s, 60 s: `SetUnitSensorRadius radarJammer` 500 and `SetUnitStealth` / `SetUnitSonarStealth` true: invisible to radar and sonar);
  - at the end the values of the unit definition come back (`A.stop`).
  - At the start `A.count()` beacons of every ability (one per 3000 x 3000 Elmo of the land and at least one per two teams, 2 to 6 of each kind, so 10 to 30) lie at random places of the land;
    - every 15 frames `A.update`: a player's unit (`owner`) within `A.PICK` = 80 Elmo that does not have that ability takes it (chat line "... found Sprint.");
    - a beacon of an ability the unit has stays, except while that ability cools down (`p.cool[kind]` in the future): then the unit uses it up, `p.cool[kind] = nil`, the chat says "... used a beacon: ... is ready again." and the beacon comes back 45 s later like a taken one;
    - a taken one lies somewhere else 45 s later, of the same kind (`A.due` holds frame and kind; no free place found: again after 5 s).
  - A player keeps its abilities until its unit dies (`A.lose`; evolving keeps them).
  - The abilities are not buttons of the order menu of the game (it hides disabled orders and knows no colors): the unsynced part draws its own bar (`H.bar`) in the own character panel and handles the mouse (`IsAbove`, `MousePress`): a click sends the game message `evo|use|<index>`, `RecvLuaMsg` starts the ability (`A.use`: ready, `UseTeamResource` of the cost).
  - A computer player takes radars and collectors in `steer`: while its team holds fewer than its share (`radars` of all divided by the players, at least 2) the nearest one that is not its team's (or an ally's) comes before wild units and enemies, unless an enemy player unit is within 600 Elmo;
    - it stands within 0.6 x `R.RANGE` until it is taken (`p.goal`);
    - measured with `pve_test.ps1 -Evo -Widget zz_aitake.lua` (speed 20, 9000 frames, a standing human): the computer took collectors, wind turbines and radars (`took a radar` lines).
  - It uses its abilities in `steer` (`A.ai`: repair below 40 % health, sprint toward a target farther than 600 Elmo, jammer and cloak with a target, the pulse without).
  - Game rule `evoBeacons` ("x,z,index" separated by /), team rule `evoAbilities` ("index:cooldown seconds:seconds the effect lasts" separated by ,).
  - The unsynced part draws a beacon (`DrawWorld`: a column of light in its color and a pulsing ring, additive) only where the own ally team sees (a spectator with full view sees all).
- **Computer teams:** fire state return fire; every 3 s an `CMD.ATTACK` on the nearest enemy player unit of the same stage or higher or the nearest wild unit of its stage or lower, whichever is nearer (in Armageddon any enemy player unit, no wild units), with nothing to attack a move to a random place when idle.
