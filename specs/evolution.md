# Evolution

A game mode of LAN Play on any map (button **Evolution** in the menu **LAN Play** of the room, gadget `evo_game.lua` and game widget `evo_gift.lua` of the extra game "Level Editor Scenarios"). Every team plays one unit that evolves through 10 stages by kills. User view: [Evolution manual](../LevelEditor/game-modes/evolution.md).

## 1. LAN Play room (`lan_lobby.lua`)

- Button **Evolution** in the menu of the button **LAN Options** (title row of the room, see [LAN Play](lan-play.md); caption "Evolution" / "Evolution on"), not in a scenario room. The host switches it (`setEvolution`): the game option `lanevolution=1` in `S.battle.modoptions` (sent to the room with `SETSCRIPTTAGS game/modoptions/lanevolution=1`, empty when off), and `syncPveGame()` because the mode needs the extra game (`needsExtraGame`). A joiner sees the state from the game options of the room and cannot change it.
- Switching it on opens a window for the host (`bigPopup(EVO_INFO)`): the rules in short and the tip about the map size (size class M or smaller, up to 16 x 16; the size classes of the lobby: M is mostly 16 x 16).
- Choosing a scenario switches it off (a scenario has its own rules).
- Start script (`buildScript`): `lanevolution=1`, `deathmode=neverend` instead of the room's value (the commanders are replaced, the game must not end for that; the mode never ends) and `StartPosType=0` (the start positions of the map: choosing a position would only hold up the start).
- Test automation: `evolution=1` in `lanlobby_cfg.txt` (the host switches it on after 10 s); `botVersion=` for the computer of `bot=`.
- Changing these rules is a change of the LAN logic: `LAN_VERSION` 16.

## 2. Gadget `evo_game.lua`

- Loaded only with the game option `lanevolution=1` (otherwise the file returns false).
- **Players:** from frame 30 to 900, every 15 frames, every team (not Gaia, not dead) with a commander (`customParams.iscommander`) joins: its units are removed (no wreck, no explosion) and a stage 1 unit stands where the commander stood. Teams without a commander (Scavengers, Raptors) stay as they are. A team without an active human player (`Spring.GetPlayerList(team, true)`, not a spectator) is a computer team.
- **Units:** `LADDERS`, three lists of 10 names (Armada, Cortex, Legion), chosen by damage per second against the default armor times health (armed ground units; artillery, water units and the Tumbleweed left out; the numbers: `test-data\tools\zz_dpsdump.lua`). Every spawn takes a random one of the names of its stage that exist in `UnitDefNames` (`stageUnit`).
- **Points:** `need(s) = max(1, floor(3 * 1.6^(s - 1) + 0.5))` from stage s to s + 1. A wild unit or tower of stage s gives `wildWorth(s) = max(1, floor(need(s) / 3 + 0.5))` (about three for the next stage); every kill (wild or player) counts `1 + R.CATCH_UP x (highest stage of the players who are in - stage of the killer)` times, `R.CATCH_UP` = 0.5 (a player on stage 1 next to one on stage 5 gets three times the points), a player unit of stage v killed by a unit of stage k gives `need(v) * max(1, 1 + v - k)` (`worth`). Points go to the team of the attacker (`UnitDestroyed` attackerTeam), never for an allied or the own unit. Several stages at once are possible; at stage 10 the points stay 0.
- **Evolving:** the new unit replaces the old one at its place (`evolve`: position, facing, full health; a human's unit keeps up to 16 orders, given again with shift).
- **Land:** at the start `buildLand` makes a grid of 128 x 128 Elmo cells where a heavy tank (Bull) can stand (`Spring.TestMoveOrder` without objects) and takes the connected piece (8 neighbours) with the most commanders (on a tie the bigger one; the list of its cells is sorted, so it is the same on every computer). Every unit spawns on it (`randomLand`): nobody is cut off by water or cliffs. A commander off that piece (another island) has its first unit put somewhere on it.
- **Death before Armageddon:** the stage goes down by one (at least 1), the points are lost, deaths + 1; after 5 s the unit comes back at `spawnPoint`: of 24 random places on the land where the unit can stand (also no unit or feature in the way) the one farthest from the units of the players.
- **Armageddon:** the first unit on stage 10 (`reachTop`): `Spring.SetGlobalLos(allyTeam, true)` for every ally team (no fog of war), the game rules `evoArmageddon` (frame) and `evoHero`, two chat lines; the unsynced part shows "ARMAGEDDON IS HERE" in big letters for 10 s and "ARMAGEDDON" in the title line. From then on a destroyed player unit does not come back: the player is out (`evoOut`), the units of its gifts blow up. **The alliance against level 10** (`setCoalition`, when a player reaches stage 10 and when one is out): as long as a player on stage 10 is in, every alliance of the start without such a player is allied with every other one of them (`Spring.SetAlly` both ways; chat line "Everybody else is allied now against ..."); when no player on stage 10 is left the alliances of the start come back ("... everybody fights on their own again"). Targets of the computer players and of the gifts and the points use `enemies(p, q)` (not the same alliance and not `AreTeamsAllied`). The last alliance of the start with a player that is not out wins (`checkWin`: `evoState` = "won by ...", `Spring.GameOver`). Others may still reach stage 10 later.
- **Gifts of stage 10:** every player who reaches stage 10 (`reachTop`, also later ones) chooses one (`evoGift` = 1 while it may): the game widget `evo_gift.lua` shows a window with three buttons (keys 1, 2, 3) and sends `evo|gift|<n>` (`RecvLuaMsg` checks the team of the player); a computer player chooses at once, a human gets a random one after 30 s. 1 **Hell on Earth**: 8 ground units of stage 6 to 8 (random factions) at random places of the land at least 900 Elmo from the enemies, for the player. 2 **Heaven on Earth**: 8 gunships (of armbrawl, armblade, armkam, corape, corcrw that exist) 250 Elmo around the player's unit. 3 **Gates of Hell**: 12 nuclear missiles (the weapon of armsilo / corsilo / legsilo) one every 4 s at random places of the land at least 1600 Elmo from the player's unit; each one is shown 5 s before by red rings (`evoNukes`, drawn by the unsynced part) and comes down with `Spring.SpawnProjectile` from 2500 Elmo above (`Spring.SpawnExplosion` when that fails). The kills of the gifts count for the player. Units of a gift of a computer team attack the nearest enemy player unit.
- **Rules table `R`** (Lua 5.1 allows only so many locals): energy, wild units, towers, radars below.
- **Energy:** every second (`giveEnergy`) every player team that is not out gets `R.ENERGY` = 5 energy per stage, at least `R.ENERGY_MIN` = 10 (`AddTeamResource`; a Razorback of stage 8 needs 5 energy per shot) and a storage of at least `R.STORE` = 1000 (`SetTeamResource "es"`; the commander and its storage are gone, laser weapons need energy).
- **Wild units:** `wildCount()` = one per 1024 x 1024 Elmo of the land, 12 to 100; Gaia units at random places, health 20 % (`SetUnitMaxHealth`), fire state return fire, move state hold position. `wild[id] = { stage, tower }`. The stage (`wildStage`): the lowest stage of the players who are not out (the weakest player can always catch up on them). A wild tower does `R.TOWER_DAMAGE` = 50 % of its damage (`UnitPreDamaged`). Every `R.WANDER` = 5 s `wanderWild` sends each idle wild unit that is not a tower with the chance `R.WANDER_SHARE` = 30 % to a random place of the land 210 to 700 Elmo away (`CMD.MOVE`; they keep holding their position there and only shoot back). With the chance `R.TOWER` = 15 % a spawn is a tower (`stageTower`: `R.TOWERS`, three lists of 10 towers per faction, ground targets, ordered by cost; anti air, nuclear and anti nuclear buildings left out; `fits` uses `TestBuildOrder` for buildings). A destroyed one comes back 10 s later somewhere else (a new stage); every `R.CHECK` = 10 s `topUpWild` fills up to `wildCount()` (living ones plus those due; ids that are gone without `UnitDestroyed` are dropped). Every `R.UPGRADE` = 3 s `upgradeWild` replaces one wild unit two or more stages away from that stage (below or above: the weakest player went down after a death) with one of that stage at its place (a tower with a tower).
- **Radars:** at the start `placeRadars`: neutral radars of Gaia (`R.RADAR1`, a random faction; `SetUnitNeutral`, nobody shoots at them on its own) until `R.COVER` = 95 % of the land cells are in their range (the radar's `radarDistance`, 2100); each new one stands in a random cell no radar reaches yet, so they keep their distance and cover the land evenly (at most `R.MAX_RADARS` = 60). They cannot be destroyed (`UnitPreDamaged` returns 0; `GG.Indestructible` of `unit_indestructible.lua` when loaded). Every 15 frames `takeRadars`: units of exactly one alliance of players within `R.RANGE` = 200 Elmo and not the owner's: after `R.TAKE` = 5 s the radar goes to the team of the unit that began (`TransferUnit`, chat line "... took a radar."); another alliance there or nobody resets it. The progress is the game rule `evoTaking` ("x,z,seconds,team" separated by /), drawn by the unsynced part as "Taking 3/5" in the taker's color above the radar. A player who is out gives their radars back to Gaia (`freeRadars`). When more than half of the players who are in are on stage `R.T2_STAGE` = 5 or higher, `upgradeRadars` replaces one radar every 3 s with the T2 radar of its faction (`R.RADAR2`, range 3500) for the same owner.
- **Abilities and beacons** (table `A`; `ABILITIES` at the top is shared with the unsynced part): five abilities (key, name, color, energy cost, seconds it lasts, cooldown): Jammer (violet, 300, 20 s, 60 s: `SetUnitSensorRadius radarJammer` 500), Cloak (blue, 400, 15 s, 60 s: `SetUnitCloak` true with decloak distance 75 and script cloak 1), Sprint (yellow, 200, 8 s, 30 s: `MoveCtrl.SetGroundMoveTypeData` maxSpeed / maxWantedSpeed x 1.5), Repair (green, 500, at once, 90 s: +30 % health), Radar Pulse (orange, 250, 5 s, 45 s: los and airLos 2500); at the end the values of the unit definition come back (`A.stop`). At the start `A.count()` beacons (one per 2048 x 2048 Elmo of the land, 5 to 20) lie at random places of the land, each of a random ability; every 15 frames `A.update`: a player's unit (`owner`) within `A.PICK` = 80 Elmo that does not have that ability takes it (chat line "... found Sprint."); a beacon of an ability the unit has stays; a taken one lies somewhere else 45 s later in a new random color. A player keeps its abilities until its unit dies (`A.lose`; evolving keeps them). Every ability is a button of the unit (`CMD_ABILITY` + index, action `evo_<key>`, `InsertUnitCmdDesc` / `EditUnitCmdDesc` in `A.buttons` after every spawn; disabled while it cools down). The order menu of the game shows the translations `commands.evo_<key>` and `commands.evo_<key>_tooltip`: the gadget puts name and explanation into the game rule `evoAbilityTexts` ("evo_<key>|name|tooltip" separated by #) and the game widget `evo_gift.lua` sets them with `BAR.I18N.set` for English and the language of the game; `AllowCommand` starts it at once (`A.use`: ready, `UseTeamResource` of the cost) and drops the order. A computer player uses its abilities in `steer` (`A.ai`: repair below 40 % health, sprint toward a target farther than 600 Elmo, jammer and cloak with a target, the pulse without). Game rule `evoBeacons` ("x,z,index" separated by /), team rule `evoAbilities` ("index:cooldown seconds:seconds the effect lasts" separated by ,). The unsynced part draws a beacon (`DrawWorld`: a column of light in its color and a pulsing ring, additive) only where the own ally team sees (a spectator with full view sees all). Not checked in a game yet: in particular whether the script cloak hides units that cannot cloak by themselves.
- **Computer teams:** fire state return fire; every 3 s an `CMD.ATTACK` on the nearest enemy player unit of the same stage or higher or the nearest wild unit of its stage or lower, whichever is nearer (in Armageddon any enemy player unit, no wild units), with nothing to attack a move to a random place when idle. `AllowCommand` drops every order to their unit that is not given by synced code (the orders of the team's own AI), except the state orders (fire state, move state, repeat, idle mode).
- **Protection:** a player's unit cannot self-destruct (`CMD.SELFD`) or be given away (`AllowUnitTransfer`).
- **Display (unsynced):** the players see "Level" for the stage (texts, chat, the gift window; the code keeps the word stage). A compact panel at the right edge below the bars of the game (`H.panel`, 330 x (24 + 37 per team) at 1080 lines, scaled with `ui_scale`), the teams sorted by stage, then points (the out ones at the bottom) at 1080 lines, scaled with `ui_scale`), drawn with plain shapes (`H.box`: boxes with round corners and a vertical gradient, `H.disc`, `H.ring`): dark glass with a thin border (red in Armageddon), the title EVOLUTION / ARMAGEDDON, one row per team: team color stripe, a round badge with the level, the name and below it a bar of the points with "2/8" (green, gold at the top level: MAX), on the right a crosshair with the kills, a skull with the deaths and below them the abilities as small squares in their colors (first letter; dimmed with the seconds while cooling down, a white border while it works); the own row lighter with a border; the own new unit is selected (`SelectUnitArray`); after a respawn the camera goes to it (`SetCameraTarget`).
- **State:** game rules `evoState` ("" / "running" / "won by ..."), `evoTeams`, `evoArmageddon`, `evoHero`, `evoNukes`, `evoTaking`, `evoBeacons`; team rules (public) `evoStage`, `evoXp`, `evoNeed`, `evoKills`, `evoDeaths`, `evoOut`, `evoUnit`, `evoSpawn`, `evoName` (unit definition name), `evoGift`, `evoAbilities`.
- **Test option:** `lanevolutionstage=<n>`: every player starts on stage n (`lan_test.ps1 -EvoStage 10`, `evolutionStage=` in `lanlobby_cfg.txt`).

## 3. Tests

`lan_test.ps1 -Mode evo` (official map, default Comet Catcher Remake 1.8): the host switches Evolution on, PlayerA, PlayerB and a BARb computer (ally team 2) play; `zz_lantest.lua` logs the state every 10 s, sends the units of the two humans to random places in the middle and takes the pictures of the README with the engine's own `screenshot png` at frames 2460, 5460 and 8460 (camera on the own unit; `screenshots\` of the host's write dir). The picture of the room: `lan_test.ps1 -Mode evoroom -Shots 70` (one instance, no game: Evolution on, the window with the rules closed at 40 s, the menu LAN Play opened at 44 s; `PrintWindow` works while the lobby is idle). `-EvoStage 10`: everybody starts on stage 10 (Armageddon at once, PlayerA chooses Gates of Hell and takes pictures of the window of the gift, of a red ring and of a missile coming down). `lan_test.ps1 -Mode evo -Solo` (one instance: the host and the computer; best with `-EvoStage 5 -QuitFrame 9000 -WaitSec 900`): `zz_evo2.lua` logs the energy, the wild units and towers and the radars and sends the own unit to take a radar while shooting at it. Not run through yet: the energy, the wild units of the players' stages, the towers and the radars are not checked in a game. Checked before: the button and the game option, the same state on both computers, points, evolving into units of other factions, death with respawn one stage lower, the wild units, BARb steered (its own orders dropped: they come with player 0 and fromSynced true) up to stage 4; with `-EvoStage 10` Armageddon (banner, no fog of war), the three gifts (the computer player chooses at once), the missiles with their rings, players out and "PlayerA is the last one standing" with `Spring.GameOver`.
