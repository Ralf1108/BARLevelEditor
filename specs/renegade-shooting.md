# Renegade: shooting and the weapon table

Part of the specification of [Renegade](renegade.md) (overview, game options, messages, tests, open points).

Which weapon is on which mouse button, how a shot is aimed, and the table and pictures of the weapons.

## Shooting and the three buttons (`shoot`)

- Like in a shooter, never a "target".
- While a button is down every weapon of its group gets the point the player aims at (`Spring.SetUnitTarget(unit, x, y, z, false, true, weaponNumber)`, a user target), shortened along the line from the unit to the point to the reach of that weapon (`shortened`), so it fires toward the crosshair where nothing is in reach, into the sky, at far mountains.
- An enemy unit under the crosshair is not chosen as a target (the earlier unit target made the weapon wait; the point does not); only the anti-air lock is (see Anti-air below).
- The target is set at once when the message arrives (`RecvLuaMsg` calls `shoot` with force) and renewed every 3 frames.
- **The turret follows the view ("aim only"):** every weapon of the three buttons that is not pressed (not anti-air, not stockpile) gets the point the player looks at as its target (shortened to its reach; `aimable`, `inp.aimed` from the first message) so the turret turns with the mouse, and its reload is held: once it is ready its `reloadFrame` is set to `BLOCK` = 1000000, so it aims but does not fire (a weapon that is reloading is left alone and blocked when ready).
- Pressing the button sets `reloadFrame` to now (`unblock`), the weapon fires as soon as it is aimed.
- Measured with `zz_ren15.lua` (Pawn): the barrel direction (`GetUnitWeaponVectors`) followed yaw 180, 90, 180 without a button and `reloadFrame` stayed 1000000 (no shot); a click: the weapon fired 2 frames after the press (the turret was aimed already), then it was held again.
- **One click is one shot:** a weapon keeps its target while its button is down (continuous fire, as fast as it reloads);
  - when the button goes up before the weapon has fired (the turret was still turning, the weapon was reloading) the target stays until the weapon has fired (its `reloadFrame` changed) or its time is up (45 frames plus the reload time, at most 8 s; `inp.hold` per weapon in `shoot`), then it is cleared;
  - a weapon that has fired is let go at once.
- A target set for one weapon number stays after `SetUnitTarget(unit, nil)`, so clearing is a stop order (`clearTargets`), which also stops a walking or flying unit; therefore it is given only while the turret does not yet follow the view (`inp.aimed` false).
- From the first message on the weapons that are not pressed get the view point as target and a held reload (aim only), so nothing keeps firing and there is no stop order (no stutter); the weapons still held get their targets again after a clear.
- Measured with `zz_ren14.lua` (a click of 3 frames, the aim far behind the facing; the deadline is now 150 frames plus the reload time, at most 390): Pawn: the target was still set 10 frames later, the weapon had fired by 90 frames and the target was cleared; Titan laser: fired, cleared; held buttons: the reload frame advances several times (continuous fire).
- Before this a short click cleared the target before the turret had turned and nothing was fired.
- Measured (Titan, Epoch, Karganeth): each button targets only its group; after the release no weapon has a target and no reload frame changes; the weapon has its target 4 to 7 frames after the click (turret and reload are the engine's); Peewee against Peewee at 150 Elmo: 370 -> 118 health in 4 s.
- Every weapon can fire at a point in the air (Total Annihilation style, a hit is luck): a Pawn fired at a point 99 Elmo up, the weapon had its target and reloaded.

**Which weapon on which button (`slotsFor`, per unit definition, cached):**
- The table: the block `WEAPON_BUTTONS` in `scripts/renegade_data.lua` (a data file next to the gadget, loaded with `VFS.Include("luaui/Include/renegade_data.lua")`; it also holds `CRUISE_ALT`, `ATTACK_ALT` and `WEAPON_PICS`; between `-- BEGIN WEAPONS` and `-- END WEAPONS`; made by `renegade_weapons.py` from the columns Group and Fire button of `renegade_weapons.csv`) lists for a unit code the weapon numbers of `L`, `R`, `M`; a Scavenger copy uses the entry of the normal unit.
  - 497 units, 1035 weapons.
- Without an entry the rule (the python proposal is the same): weapons with the same weapon definition are one group (they fire together); dummies (no damage: footsteps, `krogweaponaim`), shields and interceptors are no weapons.
  - Missiles (`MissileLauncher`, `StarburstLauncher`, `TorpedoLauncher`) and anti-air weapons count as missiles, all others as "others"; both are sorted by range.
  - With both kinds: the shortest of the others on the left button, the rest of the others on the right (the missiles take the right button when there is no second one), the missiles in the middle.
  - With one kind only: the shortest left, the next right, all further ones in the middle.
  - Census of the units with a weapon (Scavenger and Raptor copies left out; `lanrenotest` logs it): 18 units without a usable weapon, 320 with one button, 84 with two, 124 with three: three buttons do for every unit.
  - Titan: impulsion blaster left, long-range laser right, starburst rocket middle; Epoch: medium plasma (3 guns) left, heavy plasma (2) right, Ferret missiles (anti-air) middle; Thor: 2 EMP beams left, lightning right, EMP missile middle.
- **The D-gun** (weapon type `DGun`, the commanders' Disintegrator) is the exception: always on the right button; the shortest of the other groups is then on the left, everything else in the middle (Commander: laser left, Disintegrator right, the underwater laser / torpedo / anti-air missile in the middle).
  - A manual fire weapon: it gets its target with the dgun flag (`SetUnitTarget(unit, x, y, z, true, true, weapon)`) and no aim-only target before the button.
  - Measured with `zz_ren16.lua` (Armada Commander, an enemy Pawn 150 Elmo ahead): right button: the D-gun fired 4 frames after the press and the Pawn was gone; left button: the laser fired.
  - The same rule is in `renegade_weapons.py` (`propose`): a unit with a D-gun always gets the rule, entries of the csv for such units are replaced when the table is made new.
- The main weapon is no longer a concept (an earlier version had "left = the strongest, right = all others").
- **A weapon fires only when it is loaded and the button is down (`CLICK_FRAMES`):** one click used to be one shot also when the weapon was still reloading (the target stayed until it had fired: `deadline` 150 frames + the reload), so a click during a reload fired "on its own" later.
  - Now (`shoot`), when the button goes up and the weapon has not fired yet: if it is **still reloading** (`reloadFrame` in the future) the click is dropped at once (`a.deadline = f`: the hold ends, the targets are cleared, the weapon is held again);
    - if it is **loaded** the press keeps its target for at most `CLICK_FRAMES` = 6 frames (0.2 s) from the press (`a.since`), enough for a short click to fire (the turret may have to turn a little) but not for a shot "later".
  - (An earlier version of today forgot only clicks with more than 1 s of reload left, `PENDING_MAX`; the user wanted none left over.) A button that is held keeps firing as soon as the weapon is loaded.
  - Measured with `zz_ren62.lua` (Behemoth D-gun, reload 4 s, aimed at the ground): a short click on the loaded weapon fires (frame 127, reload to 246); a click with about 3 s of reload left and a click with 7 frames left are both forgotten (the reload frame is held at 1000000 afterwards); the button held through the end of the reload fires at once (frame 292).
  - Not measured in the same run: a short click after the reload (the first shot is one).
- **The D-gun follows the view too (`aimable`):** the aim-only mode (every weapon of the buttons that is not pressed gets the view point as its target and a held reload, `BLOCK`) left out the D-gun because it is fired by hand; so the Behemoth's lasers turned with the view and its D-gun only after the button (the Incinerator).
  - Now `aimable` lets the D-gun in and the target of a D-gun is given with the manual fire flag (`SetUnitTarget(u, x, y, z, true, true, i)`), with the reload held like the others, so it turns without firing; the button releases it (`unblock`).
  - Measured with `zz_ren61.lua` (Behemoth `corjugg`, looking along yaw 0 / 90 / 180 without firing): the D-gun (weapon 1) turns to 354 / 91 degrees with the view, its reload frame is 1000000 (held: it did not fire); at yaw 180 it stays at 91 because of its firing cone (the hint TURN VEHICLE says so), and two of the three lasers stay at 0 for the same reason.
  - Not measured: the commanders' D-gun (a manual fire weapon of the commander in a real fight).

## Anti-air and weapons that also hit aircraft

- `aa`: default damage <= 2 and more damage against vtol (flak, anti-air missiles), or a description with only air tokens (g2a / a2a / s2a and no x2g / x2s: the Demon's and Kagan's shoulder rockets have ground damage but are air only).
  - `hy`: damage against the ground and against vtol and a description with "g2a" or "a2a" (the Titan's laser and starburst: "g2g/g2a").
  - Both are published per unit (`renAA`, `renHY`).
- With a lock (the `unit` of the message is an enemy): `aa` weapons get it as their target (`SetUnitTarget(unit, enemy, false, true, weaponNumber)`; homing missiles), `hy` weapons only when it flies (`canFly`), otherwise the point as every weapon; `aa` weapons without a lock do not fire.
  - When the engine refuses the unit for a weapon (the Titan starburst: `SetUnitTarget` returns false) the point where the aircraft is becomes the target.
  - Measured: anti-air turret `armrl` against a hovering Brawler 250 Elmo ahead and 100 up: crosshair 3 degrees beside it -> lock, weapon target type 1, the Brawler lost health (1780 -> 1665 in 1.3 s), looking away: no lock, no fire; Titan laser (right button): lock, target type 1, the Brawler destroyed.
- **Air-only weapons by `onlyTargets` (the Karganeth):** the gadget (`slotsFor`) took a weapon for anti-air only when it did no damage to the ground or its description said "g2a / a2a / s2a" without a ground target.
  - The **Karganeth** (`corkarg`) has `corkarg_karg_shoulder` ("ShoulderRockets", range 700, damage 100 default and 150 against aircraft) with `onlyTargets = { vtol = true }` and the description of neither kind: it was an ordinary weapon on the right button that the engine never lets fire at the ground, and without the anti-air circle there was no lock (found with `zz_ren56.lua`: `renAA` was empty).
  - Now `airOnly = onlyTargets.vtol == true and onlyTargets.surface ~= true` of the unit definition also makes a group anti-air (`g.aa`); the panel shows AIR ONLY, the circle appears and the right button fires at the locked aircraft.
  - Measured: `zz_ren56.lua` (`renAA` = 2) and `zz_ren57.lua` (a copy of `zz_ren13.lua` with `corkarg`: circle, lock, the gunship loses health 1780 to 1480).
  - Other units with the same pattern are fixed by the same rule; not listed one by one.

## Arc weapons, missiles, beams, turret angle

- **Arc weapons (`lobbed`, `lobbedAll`, `R.skyShot`):** a weapon shoots in an arc when it is a missile, starburst or artillery by type, a cannon with `highTrajectory` 1, or a cannon whose shell has to leave at 20 degrees or more to reach its maximum range: sin(2a) = range x gravity / `projectilespeed`^2 >= 0.64 (gravity = `myGravity`, or the map's `Game.gravity / 900`; measured over all unit definitions, `zz_ren51.lua`: `highTrajectory` is 2 for nearly everything, so it does not tell; Mauser 0.85 = 29 degrees, Cormart 0.85, Guardian 0.78, Fido 0.73, Bulldog 0.66, Hammer 0.60 (no), Pawn 0.07 (no)).
  - They get the impact preview (disc and ring) like the missiles.
  - A unit whose weapons (the anti-air ones aside) **all** shoot in an arc can be fired at the sky: `aim` then puts `R.skyShot`, a point on the ground at 95% of the longest range along the view direction, in place of the far point in the air that no arc weapon can hit (`R.shot`; `R.hit` stays nil, so the crosshair still says "sky" and the preview is drawn at that point).
  - Measured with `zz_ren52.lua` (Mauser, `armmart`): looking at the map edge outside: `skyShot` 779 Elmo away (0.95 x 820) and the weapon reloads (it fired); looking at the ground: disc of the explosion at the crosshair.
  - Not measured: where the shell lands and the other arc units.
- **Arc weapons by their picture, and the look jump again:** `lobbed(wd, u, i)` also counts a weapon whose category picture (`renPics`) is `artillery`, `nuclear` or `emp-grenade` (`ARC_PICS`; `heavy-rocket` and `rocket-salvo` were in it for a day and are out again, see below) as an arc weapon, whatever the physics formula says: bombs, artillery, rockets and nuclear missiles always get the impact preview (and a unit with only such weapons can be fired at the sky);
  - only the flat shooters (tank guns, laser towers, gauss, heat rays) have just the crosshair.
  - **The bug "the view jumps straight up" came back** (the guard of the earlier round did not catch it, or the installed game was older): the only place that changes `R.pitch` is the mouse look in `Update` (`R.pitch + (my - cy) * SENS`, clamped to +-89; `math.min(89, nan)` would also give 89), so it is a wrong `my`.
  - The guard is stricter now: a turn of more than 35 degrees up or down, or 70 degrees sideways (233 / 467 pixels at `SENS` 0.15) in one frame is ignored as well (before: only 40% of the screen, a gap of more than 0.25 s and the first frame after the menu), and the first five ignored jumps are written to the log with the numbers: `[Renegade] look jump ignored: mouse <x> <y> middle <cx> <cy> gap <s> pitch <deg>`;
    - if the bug appears again, that line in `infolog.txt` (or none: then the cause is not the mouse jump) tells where to look.
- **Direct and indirect missiles (`lobbed`, round two):** measured over all unit definitions (`zz_ren59.lua`): a `MissileLauncher` has `trajectoryHeight` > 0 when its missile flies an arc (29 + 54 weapons: Janus, Banisher, Mercury, Screamer ...) and 0 when it flies straight at the target (120 weapons: Karganeth, Rocko, Storm, the anti-air missiles);
  - a `StarburstLauncher` climbs for `uptime` seconds and dives (Merl 4.2, Catapult 4, the nuclear missiles 2.9 to 5.5), the Titan's guided starburst climbs only 0.9 s.
  - So: **missile launcher = arc only with `trajectoryHeight` > 0; starburst = arc from `uptime` 2 s; the picture categories `artillery`, `nuclear` and `emp-grenade` and the artillery shells and the cannons by the physics rule stay arcs** (`heavy-rocket` and `rocket-salvo` no longer force it: they contain direct rockets).
  - Measured with `zz_ren60.lua` (`R.lobbed`): Karganeth missiles, shoulder rockets, Rocko, Storm, the Titan's starburst, anti-air missiles: direct (no preview); Janus, Banisher, Merl, Catapult, the nuclear missiles, Mauser, Bulldog: arc (preview).
  - **Air-only weapons, all units** (`zz_ren58.lua`, `onlyTargets.vtol` without `surface`): 170 weapons;
    - 158 were already recognised, 12 were not: the shoulder rockets of the Karganeth, the Demon and the Karganeth squad (`cordemon`, `corkarg`, `squadcorkarg`; all shoulder rockets are air-only), `corarch#2`, `corvamp#1` (an air-to-air missile), `leegmech#5` (Top Gun AA), `legvenator#1` (bird shot), the commander boss (`corcomboss` 1 and 2) and three raptor healers;
    - they are all fixed by the rule of the round before.
  - The other way round only `legkam#2` (`onlyTargets` = `notsub`, "a2a" in the description) is anti-air by the description and not by `onlyTargets`; it stays as it is.
- **Beams aimed through the target (`aim`: `hit.far`, message fields 9 - 11, `BEAMS`, `applyTarget`):** the view ray is followed through a unit: `hit.far` is the point behind it (the ground there, `TraceScreenRay` with onlyCoords, else `march`, else the sky point at `FAR`; a point outside the map is dropped).
  - The message `ren|yaw|pitch|keys|fire|x|y|z|unit|fx|fy|fz` carries it (the gadget still reads the old message without it).
  - For `BeamLaser`, `LaserCannon` and `LightningCannon` weapons `applyTarget` aims at the point behind the unit instead of its middle, so the beam goes through the unit along the view and is not aimed at a point on or in front of it; the other weapons (arcs, missiles, cannons) keep the unit position.
  - In the third person view the point behind is not used (the camera sits away from the weapons: the line weapon - far point would miss the unit), the beam is aimed at the unit as before.
  - **Not measured with a hit:** the sandbox start is 10 Elmo from the map edge, the enemy of `ren|test|1` 9 Elmo away and the ray looks at the ground beyond the map (`zz_ren75.lua` logs the point and fires, no damage could be seen); the effect on a real map is to be checked by playing.
- **Beams at far ground (`applyTarget`, `shortened` returns `cut`):** a beam weapon (`BEAMS`) at ground beyond its range is aimed at the point at the range on the line to the hit, which lies in the air over the valley;
  - the weapon sits lower than the eye, so the line to it may clip the terrain in front of the unit and the weapon refuses it (`Spring.GetUnitWeaponHaveFreeLineOfFire` false; the sky is steeper, so it worked).
  - When the point was cut and the line is not free, the point is raised in steps of 15 Elmo (up to 200) until the line is free.
  - Report of the player: the Laser Tiger (`corgatreap`, ground beam, range 620) does not fire at far mountains when the crosshair is red, in the first and the third person view.
  - **Not reproduced:** `zz_ren77.lua` (no out-of-range mountain on the test map, the cliff wall is 300 - 370 Elmo away; `GetUnitWeaponTestTarget` accepted both the mid-air point and the point on the ground, the free line was reported false for points on the cliff face although the weapon fired); the fix is a guess from the engine's rules, so it has to be checked by playing.
- **Turret angle (`outOfCone`):** a weapon that cannot turn all the way round has `maxAngleDif` (the cosine of the largest angle from its main direction; -1 = no limit; the Mauser `armmart`: -4.4e-8 = 90 degrees to each side) and `mainDirX/Y/Z` (unit frame, z = forward) in `UnitDefs[].weapons[i]` (measured with `zz_ren49.lua`; the other units tested: -1).
  - The widget compares the look direction (`GetCameraVectors().forward`) with the main direction in the world (`GetUnitVectors`: front, up, right are three tables).
  - Outside of the cone: the row of the weapon is red with "TURN VEHICLE >>" or "<< TURN VEHICLE" (the side of the target), and when **no** weapon of the unit can point where you look the crosshair turns red with the text "TURN THE VEHICLE >> (outside of the turret angle)" (`R.coneOut` for the tests).
  - Measured with `zz_ren50.lua` (Mauser: yaw 180 outside = hint, yaw 0 inside = none; the side of the arrows is not checked).
- **Rattlesnake and the smart weapon selection (found in the BAR files, fixed):** the source of the unit script `scripts/units/armamb.bos`, `scripts/scavs/smart_weapon_select.h`, `scripts/popup_openclose.h` and of the gadget `luarules/gadgets/unit_weapon_smart_select_helper.lua` can be read from the BAR archive (the `.sdp` index in `data/packages`, the files in `data/pool/<2 hex>/<rest>.gz`).
  - Eight units have `customParams.weapons_smart_select` (`zz_smart.lua`): Rattlesnake `armamb`, Gauntlet `armguard`, Vanguard `armvang`, Agitator `corpun`, Persecutor `cortoast`, Eviscerator `legacluster`, Amputator `legcluster`, Myrmidon `legeallterrainmech`;
    - all have weapon 1 = low trajectory (`smart_priority`), 2 = high trajectory (`smart_backup`), 3 = a dummy (`smart_trajectory_checker`, no damage).
  - They are **one weapon**: the script of each aim function refuses (`return 0`) unless the unit's `AimingState` is its own (the gadget switches it once a second from the line of fire of the dummy, copying the target of weapon 1 to weapon 2), and **also refuses while the OTHER weapon is "reloading"** (`get GAME_FRAME < get WEAPON_RELOADSTATE(other)`: "no bonus shots").
  - Our aim-only mode holds every weapon that is not pressed with a reload frame of 1000000, so pressing one button (weapon 1 or weapon 2 alone) was never enough: the other one still looked as if it was reloading for a million frames.
  - Only both buttons together unblocked both: "if you click left and right wildly it shoots now and then".
  - The pop-up cannon (`popup_openclose.h`: open when it aims, closes after `restoreDelay` 10 s without a target; `BlockShot` = not open) was not the cause (it was open in the first 10 s of the test).
  - **Fix (second version, the user wanted both kinds):** the two weapons are **two buttons again**: weapon 1 (low trajectory, "direct") on the left button, weapon 2 (high trajectory, "vertical") on the right button, as the table says.
  - When a smart weapon is pressed the gadget (`smartOf`, in `shoot`) (1) gives the unit the order `GameCMD.SMART_TOGGLE` with 0 (low) or 1 (high), the order of BAR's gadget `unit_weapon_smart_select_helper` that sets the aiming state of the script at once and leaves a fixed state alone (2 = automatic), and (2) releases the held reload of the **partner** as well (`unblock`; the aim-only loop does not hold the partner of a pressed smart weapon), so the script no longer sees the other weapon as reloading;
    - the partner has no target and does not fire.
  - The panel shows the two rows as "...
  - (direct)" and "...
  - (vertical)" (`customParams.smart_priority` / `smart_backup`).
  - Measured with `zz_ren67.lua`: the left button fires weapon 1 (3 frames after the press, reload to 199); the right button fires weapon 2 (62 frames after the press: the aiming state switches and the turret turns; reload to 519); before, no weapon fired in 5 s with any order, state or target.
  - Not measured: the other seven units, a wall in front of the target (with a fixed state the unit does not choose by itself any more: the player chooses), the state after letting go (it stays at the last one).

## The weapon table (`game-modes/renegade_weapons.csv`, `renegade-weapons.md`)

- **The csv is the source** (semicolons, UTF-8 with BOM, open it in Excel and import it with "Semicolon" only as the separator, no "Space", text columns as text: "-" becomes a number otherwise): the columns Faction, Unit, Code, #, Weapon (description), Type, Target, Range, Reload, Damage, Weapon name, **Fire button**, **Weapon image**.
  - Fire button = LMB, RMB, MMB or empty (weapons of a unit with the same button fire together and make one row of the panel; an old "-" or "-0" counts as empty); Weapon image = empty for now (a picture of the weapon for the panel, for when the interface is made nicer; not read by the game).
  - Target = what the weapon hits (empty = nothing; **no cell of a csv holds "-" for "empty"**: a spreadsheet takes it for a formula or a number, an empty cell stays empty): the tokens of the description (g2g, g2a, a2g, g2s ...) count first, the damage against aircraft alone does not (many ground missiles have the same damage against every armor class: Janus, Ambassador), without tokens the damage and the type.
- `game-modes/update_renegade_weapons.bat` (`renegade_weapons.py update`) writes the table into the md **only between the comment lines `BEGIN TABLE` and `END TABLE`** (the text around may be edited; a file without the markers is made whole once) and the block `WEAPON_BUTTONS` into the gadget.
  - `renegade_weapons.py table <infolog>` makes the csv new from the dump of `zz_weapondump.lua` (`[zzwep]` lines; the Fire button and Weapon image of rows that are in the csv are kept, new rows get the proposal); `renegade_weapons.py lua` writes only the block.
- **One table for the weapons and their categories (`renegade_weapons.csv`):** the separate table `renegade_weapon_categories.csv` is gone;
  - its content is in the columns of `renegade_weapons.csv`: **Category** (e.g. `laser-light`), **Weapon image** (the file name, `weapon_<category>.png`; the column was empty and unused before), **Tech** (the tech level of the unit, from `renegade_unit_tech.csv`) and **DPS** (damage of one shot / reload, rounded; the damage itself is the column Damage as before).
  - The four columns are worked out by `weapon_categories.py` (it rewrites the csv in place and does not touch the others, above all not Fire button; a weapon without a button and no anti-nuke has all four empty); `renegade_weapons.py update` and `table` read the picture from the csv (`table` runs `weapon_categories.main` itself after it has written the rows).
  - Order in `update_renegade_weapons.bat`: `weapon_categories.py` first (fills the csv, writes `renegade-weapon-categories.md`), then `renegade_weapons.py update` (the table page without the file-name column, the gadget blocks; `WEAPON_PICS` came out the same as before).
  - Do not change the four columns in Excel: the next run overwrites them (change the limits in `renegade_weapon_limits.csv` or the rules in `weapon_categories.py`).

## Pictures of the weapons

- **Pictures of the weapons (panel):** the categories of `renegade-weapon-categories.md` (made by `test-data/generators/weapon_categories.py`) get a picture each: `LevelEditor/game-modes/weapon-images/weapon_<category>.png` (the picture of a whole panel row, 6 : 1, 768 x 128 is what `weapon_art.py` writes: the weapon and its beam over the whole width on a dark background; drawn by `test-data/generators/weapon_art.py` with Pillow and numpy, or delivered from outside: any png with that name).
  - All 39 categories have a picture: the lasers and the heat ray (below) and, drawn by `test-data/generators/weapon_art2.py` (a launcher or gun with its projectile far to the right, the trail runs from the muzzle: the same position in every picture so that the rows of the panel read as one column), `cannon-*` (a shell with muzzle flash and smoke), `missile-*` (a white rocket with red nose and fins, a single tube), `heavy-rocket` (starburst: a rocket and its burst of bomblets), `rocket-salvo` (a round rocket pod with 19 openings, seven small rockets), `plasma-*` (a violet ball with a trail of smaller balls) and `torpedo` (in water, bubbles only behind it);
    - the sizes light, medium, heavy and ultra.
  - The beam weapons: `laser-heavy` (green beam), `laser-medium` (blue), `laser-light` (red), `laser-ultra` (the heaviest: blue, a very thick beam; tachyon weapons, "ultra" in the description or 2500 damage and more: the Pulsar, the Bulwark's tachyon accelerator; 14 weapons) and `heatray` (yellow, thick; "heat ray" in the description: the Juggernaut, the Adjudicator, Thermite, Armageddon; 27 weapons; EMP beams are `stun-beam`).
  - Measured: the Pulsar shows `1=laser-ultra`, the Juggernaut `2=heatray` (`zz_ren44.lua`);
